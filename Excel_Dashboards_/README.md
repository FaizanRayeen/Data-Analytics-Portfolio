## 🛠️ Tools & Technical Highlights

* **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts**: Dynamic grouping, aggregation, and multi-dimensional analysis.
* **Interactive Slicers**: Seamless filtering across store locations, customer segments, and product categories.
* **Excel Formulas**: `VLOOKUP`, `INDEX-MATCH`, `SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `ROUND`.
* **Executive Dashboard Design**: Professional color palettes, structured layout grids, and KPI cards.
