## 📌 Executive Summary

Welcome to my central **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Portfolio**. This repository consolidates end-to-end analytical solutions built using **PostgreSQL SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and Microsoft Excel**.

Each project is designed around a real-world business case—focusing on customer retention, revenue optimization, logistical efficiency, financial risk, and audience intelligence.

### 🌟 Key Portfolio Highlights
* 📁 **9 Portfolio Projects** across 4 key analytical domains (E-Commerce, Banking, Food Logistics, HR, Entertainment).
* 🧹 **50,000+ Data Rows** cleaned, transformed, and modeled.
* 📊 **6+ Interactive Dashboards** built with Power BI, Tableau, and Excel.
* 🔍 **40+ Advanced SQL Queries** leveraging CTEs, window functions (`RANK`, `DENSE_RANK`, `SUM OVER`), and multi-table JOINs.

## 📂 Comprehensive Module Breakdown

### 📗 [Excel Dashboards](./Excel_Dashboards_)
> Dynamic Excel dashboards leveraging Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and advanced formulas.
* ☕ **[Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard](./Excel_Dashboards_/Coffee-Shop-Sales-Dashboard)** — Sales trends, product performance, and store-wise revenue breakdown.
* 📉 **[Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard](./Excel_Dashboards_/Customer-Churn-Analysis-Dashboard)** — Customer retention metrics, contract length analysis, and churn risk identification.
* 🌍 **[Global E-Commerce Sales Dashboard](./Excel_Dashboards_/Global-Ecommerce-Sales-Dashboard)** — Regional performance, category revenue split, and net profit margins.

---

### 📘 [Power BI Dashboards](./Powerbi_Dashboards_)
> Executive Power BI reports utilizing DAX measures, data modeling, decomposition trees, and multi-page layouts.
* 🎬 **[Netflix Content Intelligence Dashboard](./Powerbi_Dashboards_/Netflix-Content-Intelligence-Dashboard)** — Content distribution, rating trends, genre insights, and country production maps.
* 🏦 **[Bank Performance Dashboard](./Powerbi_Dashboards_/Bank-Performance-Dashboard)** — Multi-page financial metrics, customer risk profiling, digital fraud rates, and loan portfolio health.
* ☕ **[From Beans to Business: Coffee Shop Analysis](./Powerbi_Dashboards_/Data-Driven-Coffee-Shop-Analysis)** — 5-page report featuring root-cause decomposition trees and peak sales hour analysis.

---

### 📙 [Tableau Dashboards](./Tableau_Dashboards_)
> Interactive visual storytelling using calculated fields, dashboard actions, and custom chart types.
* 👥 **[HR Analytics & Attrition Dashboard](./Tableau_Dashboards_/HR-Analytics-Attrition-Dashboard)** — Workforce demographics, department-wise attrition drivers, monthly income correlation, and retention insights.

---

### 🗃️ [PostgreSQL SQL Projects](./SQL_Projects_)
> Advanced relational database design and analytical SQL query suites built on PostgreSQL.
* 🛒 **[E-Commerce Sales Analytics](./SQL_Projects_/Ecommerce-Postgres-Analytics)** — 20 analytical queries across 5 relational tables addressing revenue growth, top spenders, and order status distribution.
* 🍔 **[Food Delivery Analytics](./SQL_Projects_/Food-Delivery-SQL-Analytics)** — Platform analytics covering restaurant revenue ranking (`DENSE_RANK`), delivery SLA classification (`CASE WHEN`), and customer RFM behavior.
