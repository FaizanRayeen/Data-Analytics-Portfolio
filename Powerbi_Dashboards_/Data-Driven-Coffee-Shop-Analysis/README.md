## 📊 Business Overview & Objectives

Retail coffee chains must balance inventory, staff scheduling during rush hours, beverage pricing strategies, and payment processing preferences.

### Core Objectives:
* 💰 Analyze **Total Sales Revenue ($1M+)** across 10,000+ customer orders.
* 🌲 Perform root-cause sales analysis using Power BI **Decomposition Trees**.
* ⏰ Identify peak hourly sales windows (Morning vs. Afternoon vs. Evening).
* 💳 Evaluate customer payment preferences (UPI, Credit Card, Cash).

## 📖 Report Structure (5 Pages)

![Additional Report Pages](additional_report_pages.png)

1. **Cover & Executive Summary**: Key metrics overview and store selection.
2. **Product & Store Revenue**: Category breakdown across Gourmet Brewed Coffee, Barista Espresso, etc.
3. **Revenue Trends Over Time**: Hourly, daily, and monthly growth trends.
4. **Payment Modes & Demographics**: Payment breakdown segmented by gender and customer type.
5. **Sales Driver Decomposition Tree**: AI-powered root cause analysis tree detailing revenue drivers.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Morning Rush Window**: Morning hours (7 AM – 11 AM) generate **61.91% of total daily revenue**.
2. **Top Product Categories**: *Gourmet Brewed Coffee, Barista Espresso, and Premium Brewed Coffee* represent the top 3 revenue generators.
3. **Payment Preferences**: **UPI and Digital Payments** account for over 65% of total sales, outperforming Cash transactions.
